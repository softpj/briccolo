Privacy,
in plain words.

Briccolo is preparing for its first App Store release. This page describes the game’s local storage and its planned advertising and purchase features; live ads and purchases are not yet enabled.

Contact

This policy describes Briccolo (previously called Ricochet during development) for iPhone and iPad. For support, privacy questions or requests, contact the Briccolo developer at softpj@icloud.com.

Game progress and settings

Briccolo stores level progress, stars, best results, unfinished runs and preferences on your device. The current version has no game-account sign-up or developer-operated progress-sync server.

Advertising

The free edition shows a banner above the playfield during gameplay. A full-screen ad may appear when continuing after every three newly completed levels. Replaying cleared levels does not count toward this cadence. A verified Remove Ads purchase disables both formats.

The free edition is planned to use Google AdMob. The integration requests non-personalized ads and applies child-directed treatment and a general-audience ad-content limit to all players, without collecting ages or dates of birth. This does not mean no data is processed: Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, device identifiers, diagnostic information, advertising data and interactions for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on consent, device permissions and the final ad configuration.

Where required, the app presents privacy choices and provides an ad-privacy option in Settings. A verified Remove Ads purchase stops future advertising requests from the game. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s iOS ad SDK data disclosures.

Children and families

Briccolo is intended for all ages. The game does not ask children to create accounts, submit contact details or share their location. Advertising requests receive child-directed treatment for every player. This disables interest-based advertising and remarketing in Google’s ad service; it does not eliminate processing needed to deliver, secure and measure ads. Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy or support. Children should ask an adult before emailing support or making a purchase.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. Briccolo uses StoreKit to check purchase status and provide the ad-free entitlement. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Resetting game progress does not cancel an Apple purchase. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include. We use those messages to answer your request and resolve problems. Avoid sending sensitive information that isn’t needed for support.

Your choices

You can reset local game progress in Settings. Purchase records remain with Apple. You can review device privacy permissions in iOS Settings and use the app’s ad-privacy choices when available. Contact us for requests involving support messages you have sent.

This website

This website uses no developer-added advertising, analytics scripts or forms. The hosting provider may process connection and request information needed to serve and protect the site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Changes

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change, including any future analytics, cloud saves or advertising partners.
