Easy to start. Worth mastering.

Your machine.
One press.

  1. Drag pieces from your tray anywhere on the board. Twist the red handle to turn a piece in 5° steps.
  2. Press Run. The marble drops from its brass dropper and rolls through your machine.
  3. Get the job done: toast on the plate, water in the pot, fish fed. Do it at par, with the fewest pieces it needs, and catch the gem for three stars.

Follow the cables: a switch can pop the toaster, fire the cannon or open a trapdoor. Conveyor belts, fans, glass pipes, turning paddles and padlocks join in from room to room. Most trays include a piece you won’t need. Everything works offline.
