How do I play?

Every machine is nearly finished: the grey steel is the machine, and the pieces in your tray fill its gaps. Drag a piece anywhere on the board, or tap a piece and then a spot. Tap a placed piece to select it and twist its red handle to turn it in 5° steps; drag it to move it, or drop it back on the tray to take it off. Press Run to start the machine and Stop to go back to building. The job is done when the toast lands on the plate, the plant gets its water, the cans fall, the ball reaches the basket or the fish is fed. Tap 💡 Hint to see where the next piece goes.

How do I get three stars?

One star for getting the job done, one for doing it at par (the fewest pieces it needs; most trays hold a piece you won’t need) and one for catching the 💎 gem on the way. Using a hint caps a job at two stars.

What do the cables and special switches mean?

A cable runs from a switch to the machinery it works, and a spark runs along it when the switch is pressed: the toaster pops, the cannon fires or the trapdoor opens. A HEAVY switch ignores marbles: only the big ball is heavy enough. BLUE ONLY and TOAST ONLY switches answer to nothing else. A padlock needs all of its switches pressed.

How do I start a job over or choose another?

Tap Rebuild this one (or Try for ★★★) on the results screen, or open Menu → Start this machine over. Menu → Level select returns to the job board.

How does Remove Ads work?

The planned one-time purchase disables every ad. In the released app, open Settings → Remove Ads. Apple displays the current price for your storefront before you confirm.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the same Apple Account used to purchase, open Settings → Remove Ads → Restore purchases. A progress reset doesn’t remove an active ad-free purchase.

Does Briccolo work offline?

The jobs and original music are bundled with the game. Advertising, new purchases and some store checks need a connection. A missing ad never blocks the next job.

Will my progress sync between devices?

The current version saves progress on each device. Automatic cross-device progress sync is not included. Apple purchases can be restored with the Apple Account used to buy them.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem page.

When do ads appear?

The free edition shows a banner above the playfield during gameplay. A full-screen ad may appear when you continue after every three newly completed jobs. Replaying finished jobs does not count toward the next ad, and ads never interrupt a running machine. Remove Ads disables both formats.
